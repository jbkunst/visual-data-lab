Choose a distribution and drag its parameters. **Theoretical** shows population results; **Simulated** uses 1,000 incomes. **New sample** updates all charts.

Lorenz shows cumulative income shares, with Gini in its legend. Uniform incomes spread across a range are unequal: Beta(1,1) has Gini 1/3. Equal incomes give the diagonal; concentrated distributions approach it. The heatmap compares 50 groups of 2%. Income by percentile reveals the upper tail.
